📚 Quick Cheat Sheet

Physics

  • Kinematics: $v = u + at$, $S = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2$, $v^2 = u^2 + 2aS$
  • Energy: $KE = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$, $PE = mgh$
  • Optics: $\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} - \frac{1}{u}$

Chemistry

  • Ideal Gas Law: $PV = nRT$
  • First Law of Thermo: $\Delta U = q + w$
  • Arrhenius Equation: $k = Ae^{-E_a/RT}$

Mathematics

  • Quadratic Formula: $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$
  • Integration: $\int_{0}^{a} f(x)dx = \int_{0}^{a} f(a-x)dx$
  • Taylor Series: $e^x = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^n}{n!}$
